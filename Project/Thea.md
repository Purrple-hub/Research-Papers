# 📚 Thea – AI‑Powered Study Tool

> **Transform any course material into interactive study tools in seconds. Join millions of students worldwide who are earning better grades with less stress.** 

Thea is an AI‑powered personalized learning platform that turns your lecture notes, PDFs, handwritten pages, and even YouTube videos into flashcards, quizzes, practice tests, and smart study guides. Instead of handing you the answers, Thea *asks you the questions* — a Socratic approach designed to build genuine understanding rather than encourage shortcuts.

---

## 📋 Project Description

Founded in Arlington, Virginia, **Thea Study** is on a mission to replace passive content consumption with active, engaging practice. The platform has already delivered over **one billion practice questions** across more than **200 countries and territories** in under three years.

Thea isn't another AI tool that gives you the answer and moves on. It's a **transformational** study partner: you upload your materials, Thea extracts the key concepts, and then challenges you with adaptive questions that target exactly what you need to work on. It's research‑backed, mobile‑first, and completely free during its beta phase.

### Research‑Backed Methodology

Thea combines proven learning techniques:

- **Active Recall** – You're asked questions, not given answers
- **Spaced Repetition** – Information is reviewed at optimal intervals for long‑term retention
- **Question Variety** – Multiple‑choice, open‑response, matching, ordering, diagram labeling, and more
- **Adaptive Difficulty** – Questions get harder as you improve
