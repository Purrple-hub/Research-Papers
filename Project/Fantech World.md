# Fantech World: The Rise of a Global Gaming Peripherals Powerhouse from Southeast Asia

---

## Abstract

Fantech World has emerged as one of the most significant success stories in the global gaming peripherals industry, transforming from a modest three-brother startup in Indonesia in 2009 into a multinational enterprise with distribution spanning more than 80 countries and a community exceeding five million users. This paper provides a comprehensive examination of Fantech World as a company, a brand, and a cultural phenomenon within the gaming ecosystem. We analyze the company's historical evolution, organizational structure, dual-headquarters strategy, extensive product portfolio spanning gaming mice, mechanical keyboards, audio headsets, gaming chairs, controllers, and lifestyle accessories, its technological innovations including ultra-lightweight designs, high-performance sensors, and Hall-Effect components, its strategic collaborations with major intellectual properties such as One Piece and Hololive, its esports sponsorship initiatives, community engagement through Discord and other platforms, and its competitive positioning within the global peripherals market. Drawing on company documentation, industry analysis, product reviews, and community data, we identify the key factors that have enabled Fantech's rapid growth from an Indonesian brand to a global force, the tradeoffs inherent in its value-for-money positioning, and the company's role in democratizing access to high-performance gaming gear. The evidence demonstrates that Fantech has successfully carved out a distinct identity in a crowded market by combining aggressive innovation, strategic partnerships, and a deep understanding of its core Southeast Asian fanbase while steadily expanding its global footprint.

**Keywords:** Fantech World, gaming peripherals, esports, mechanical keyboards, gaming mice, Indonesian technology brand, One Piece collaboration, Hololive collaboration, gaming chairs, Hall-Effect sensors, ultra-lightweight gaming mice

Fantech World represents one of the most compelling case studies in this regard.

The brand's trajectory from obscurity to global recognition offers valuable insights into how a company from an emerging market can compete with established Western and Asian giants in the technology sector.

## 2. History and Founding

### 2.1 The Three Brothers and the Indonesian Origin

Fantech was established in 2009 in Jakarta, Indonesia, by three brothers: Fandy Pinardy, Ferdy Pinardy, and Firsandy Pinardy. The company's name, FANTECH, is a portmanteau of "Fantastic" and "Technology," reflecting the founders' ambition to combine cool product design with the latest technological innovations.

The founding story is rooted in a simple but powerful vision: to create gaming gear that would help gamers "gear up and win". The three brothers identified a gap in the market for affordable yet high-quality gaming peripherals, particularly in Southeast Asia where established international brands were often priced beyond the reach of many consumers.

Fandy Pinardy, who serves as the company's CEO, led the venture from its earliest days. The brothers' complementary skills—spanning product development, business operations, and marketing—provided a strong foundation for the company's early growth.

### 2.2 Early Growth and Expansion

In its first years, Fantech focused on building a presence in the Indonesian domestic market. The company's value proposition was straightforward: deliver gaming peripherals with features comparable to premium brands at prices accessible to a broader audience. This approach resonated strongly with Indonesian gamers, who had long faced a choice between expensive imported gear and low-quality alternatives.

The company's growth accelerated as it expanded beyond Indonesia into other Southeast Asian markets. By 2018, Fantech had established itself as a significant player in the region, with its products gaining recognition in countries including Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia, and Thailand.

### 2.3 Key Milestones and Timeline

Fantech's development has been marked by several significant milestones:

- **2009**: Company founded in Jakarta, Indonesia, by the three Pinardy brothers.
- **2018**: Fantech's sponsored PointBlank team placed second in the PBGC competition.
- **2019**: Fantech received the Indonesia Top Brand Award.
- **2019**: The company renovated its flagship store in Indonesia.
- **2019**: Fantech introduced its first "HELIOS" mouse.
- **2019**: The "Sakura Edition" colorway was introduced.
- **2020**: Fantech became a popular brand for "work from home" and "online school" equipment during the pandemic.
- **2020**: The company launched its digital marketing strategy.
- **2021**: Fantech opened its first flagship store in China, following locations in Indonesia and the Philippines.
- **2021**: Fantech introduced its first "MAXFIT" keyboard series.
- **2022**: Fantech opened its second flagship store in China.
- **2022**: MAXFIT67 received a "Highly Recommended" award from TechPowerUp.
- **2022**: XD3V2 received "Highly Recommended" and "High Value" awards from TechPowerUp.
- **2025-2026**: The company celebrated its 17th anniversary with promotions and activations across multiple markets.

### 2.4 Evolution of the Brand Identity

Fantech's brand identity has evolved significantly over its history. The company's iconic "Wings" logo symbolizes its commitment to helping gamers reach their full potential. The slogan "Gear Up and Win" embodies the brand's promise to stand alongside gamers at every stage of their journey.

The brand has positioned itself not merely as a provider of gaming equipment but as a companion to gamers. As stated on the company's About Us page, Fantech's vision "extends beyond gaming—to enhance the productivity and creativity of the next generation by delivering innovative, accessible, and affordable technology".

The company's mission is built upon three key principles:
- Creating Adaptive Products – Designing solutions inspired by the needs and feedback of the global gaming community.
- Innovating for Everyone – Ensuring the latest technologies and advancements remain accessible to all segments of society.
- Becoming a Trusted Companion – Striving to go beyond products, by building long-term trust and reliability with users worldwide.

## 3. Organizational Structure and Global Footprint

### 3.1 Dual Headquarters Strategy

Fantech operates with a dual headquarters structure, maintaining offices in both Indonesia and China. The Indonesian headquarters, located in Jakarta, serves as the company's operational and cultural center, reflecting its origins and continued strong ties to the Southeast Asian market. The Chinese headquarters, located in Guangzhou, provides proximity to manufacturing and supply chain resources, with the company maintaining a factory and warehouse in Dongguan.

This dual-headquarters strategy provides Fantech with several advantages:
- Access to China's extensive manufacturing ecosystem.
- Proximity to key suppliers and component manufacturers.
- Ability to manage quality control and production more effectively.
- Presence in two of the world's largest gaming markets.

## 5. Technological Innovation and Product Differentiation

### 5.1 Sensor Technology

Fantech has consistently adopted industry-leading sensor technology across its premium product lines. The PixArt 3395 sensor, featured in models like the Helios II XD3V3 and Aria XD7, offers 26,000 DPI with 99.8% accuracy. The PixArt 3389 sensor in the Helios UX3V2 delivers up to 400 IPS tracking and 50G acceleration. The PixArt 3335 sensor in the Helios XD3 provides 16,000 DPI with 403 IPS tracking.

The company has also pushed into ultra-high DPI territory, with recent product launches in Chile featuring mice with 42,000 DPI.

### 5.2 Ultra-Lightweight Design

Weight reduction has been a major focus for Fantech's engineering team. The Helios II series achieves weights as low as 55 grams, while the Aria XD7 weighs 59 grams. These ultra-lightweight designs are achieved through:

- Honeycomb shell designs that remove plastic without compromising structural integrity.
- Advanced materials selection.
- Optimized internal component layout.

The company has published educational content explaining the benefits of lightweight mice, noting that "by removing plastic from the body of the mouse, manufacturers can shave off a few grams of weight, which makes a big difference in a fast-paced game where quick movements and reflexes are key".

### 5.3 Polling Rate Innovation

Fantech has pushed polling rate boundaries significantly. The Tanto S supports up to 8,000 Hz polling rate, while the Helios II Pro S supports up to 8K polling rate. The Helios II Pro includes a 4K polling rate dongle. These high polling rates provide smoother cursor movement and reduced input lag, critical for competitive gaming.

### 5.4 Switch Technology

Fantech uses high-quality switches from Huano across many of its products. The Helios II XD3V3 supports 100 million clicks, while the Helios II Pro S supports 80 million clicks. The Aria XD7 uses Huano switches.

### 5.5 Hall-Effect Technology

Fantech has adopted Hall-Effect sensor technology in its controller lineup. The EOS PRO WGP15 and NOVA II WGP16 feature Hall-Effect analog and triggers. Hall-Effect sensors use magnetic fields rather than physical contact, providing:

- Greater durability (no physical wear on potentiometers).
- Higher precision.
- Reduced drift over time.

### 5.6 Connectivity Options

Fantech products offer extensive connectivity options:

- **Tri-mode connectivity**: Wired USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth 5.3, featured in keyboards like the MAXFIT AIR99 and headsets like the STUDIO PRO.
- **Quad-mode connectivity**: Wired 3.5mm TRRS to USB-C, wired USB-A to USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth, featured in the STELLAR WHG05 headset.
- **Multi-platform compatibility**: Support for Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, Nintendo Switch, and Raspberry Pi.

### 5.7 Customization Software

Fantech provides software for product customization across its lineup. The Fantech Studio software allows users to:

- Adjust DPI settings.
- Customize RGB lighting.
- Configure macros.
- Adjust scrolling speed.
- Set EQ for audio products.
- Real-time monitoring.

The company provides firmware updates through its website, with update tools available for specific products like the Aria.

### 5.8 DIY Community Support

Fantech has demonstrated notable support for the DIY gaming community. The company provides .stp and .fbx files for products like the Helios XD5, allowing users to 3D-print custom rear covers. This level of community support is uncommon among gaming peripherals brands and reflects Fantech's engagement with enthusiast communities.

Fantech World has emerged as one of the most significant success stories in the global gaming peripherals industry. From humble beginnings in 2009 as a three-brother startup in Jakarta, the company has grown into a multinational enterprise with dual headquarters, six flagship stores, distribution across 80+ countries, and a community of over five million users.

1. **Aggressive innovation**: Developing over 100 new products annually and rapidly adopting cutting-edge technologies like high-DPI sensors, ultra-lightweight designs, and Hall-Effect components.

4. **Deep community engagement**: Building active communities on Discord and other platforms, supporting esports, and engaging with the DIY community.

5. **Regional strength**: Maintaining a strong presence in Southeast Asia while expanding globally.

6. **Comprehensive portfolio**: Offering over 300 SKUs across 15 categories, from gaming mice and keyboards to chairs, controllers, and lifestyle accessories.

The tradeoffs inherent in Fantech's value positioning—occasional quality inconsistencies, less mature software, and limited availability in some markets—are accepted by a large and growing user base that values accessibility and performance at an accessible price point.

Fantech has fundamentally altered expectations around gaming peripherals in Southeast Asia and beyond. Before the company's rise, access to competitive-grade gaming gear was often limited by price and availability. The company's future trajectory will depend on its ability to navigate the challenges of global expansion, intensifying competition, and evolving user expectations. In an industry increasingly dominated by established giants, Fantech has proven that a brand from an emerging market can compete, innovate, and win on the global stage. The company's journey from a modest startup in Jakarta to a global force in gaming peripherals offers lessons for entrepreneurs, technologists, and business leaders across the developing world.
